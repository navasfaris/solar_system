About this site

This is a free educational project that helps you build a feel for the scale and variety of our solar system. Every planet page is generated from one data set, so you can switch worlds and compare them side by side.

Values are rounded and approximate. Moon counts are confirmed moons as of early 2025 and change as astronomers make new discoveries. The orbit view is a teaching diagram: distances and speeds are compressed so that all eight planets fit on one screen.

Built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No tracking, no external requests.
